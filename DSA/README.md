## 📝 Problem Solving Guidelines

1. **Before writing code**:
   - Write out the brute force solution and its time/space complexity.
   - Look for bottlenecks (e.g. repeated searching $\to$ use hash map or two pointers).
   - Trace at least 2 small test cases and 1 edge case on paper/comments.
2. **Use the starter templates**:
   - Python: [`DSA/templates/problem_template.py`](file:///d:/zaidsystem/02_Coding/04_Learning/DSA/templates/problem_template.py)
   - C++: [`DSA/templates/problem_template.cpp`](file:///d:/zaidsystem/02_Coding/04_Learning/DSA/templates/problem_template.cpp)
3. **Log the problem**:
   - Add a row to [`TRACKER.md`](file:///d:/zaidsystem/02_Coding/04_Learning/TRACKER.md) when solved.
